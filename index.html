01 / About

Albilad Holding Company is a Saudi business group focused on logistics, transportation, heavy equipment rental, building materials trading and equipment spare parts, with an operating mindset built around quality, safety and long-term partnerships.

Headquartered in Jeddah, the group is structured to support project teams and procurement functions with practical, dependable industrial services.
